ShortyReelFactory

A private, single-user tool for publishing short vertical videos.

ShortyReelFactory helps its owner prepare short videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It runs on the owner's personal computer and is not offered as a public service; only accounts that the owner explicitly connects are used.

How it uses your account

  • YouTube: uploads videos and sets their thumbnails on the channel you authorize.
  • Instagram / TikTok: publishes Reels / videos to the account you connect.

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account permissions, Instagram or TikTok settings.
