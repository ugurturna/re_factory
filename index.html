Features

Everything a short needs, done for you.

From the first idea to the published post — while you stay in control of every step.

🧭

Shot planning

Turns a short description into a shot list with on-screen titles, places and a matching style.

🎥

Real footage first

Prefers real, high-quality footage and photos, checks every shot visually, and only fills gaps with generated clips.

🎵

Cuts on the beat

Picks a track — or the one you name — and lines up every cut with the rhythm of the music.

🎞

Copy a format

Show a video you like and get the same pacing, transitions and text style with a completely new theme.

🖼

Thumbnail & captions

Generates the cover, title, a description with location notes and the right hashtags for each platform.

✏️

Revise in plain words

“Slower”, “swap shot 3”, “use this song” — every revision is applied and kept in the history.

How it works

Four steps. One message to start.

Nothing is produced or posted without your approval.

Describe

Write a theme, a list or a reference link — from your phone or the dashboard.

Approve the plan

Review the proposed shots, titles and sources before anything is made.

Preview & revise

Watch the finished vertical video and ask for changes in plain words.

Publish

Send it to one or all of your connected channels with a single tap.
