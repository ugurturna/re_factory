Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ShortyReelFactory ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool. This policy explains what data the app accesses when you connect an account and how that data is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with your consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set thumbnails on the YouTube channel you authorize, and to read that channel's basic information (channel name and ID). It does not read your email, contacts, comments, watch history or any other data.
  • Instagram (Meta): your account ID and username, and permission to publish Reels to that account.
  • TikTok: your basic profile (open ID, display name) and permission to post videos to that account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish videos that you choose to publish to your own accounts. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. The app has no central server and does not send your data to any third party other than the platform you are publishing to (Google/YouTube, Meta, TikTok).

Sharing

Data is not shared with, transferred to or sold to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ShortyReelFactory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account is connected. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access at any time at Google security settings, or in your Instagram / TikTok app settings; revoked tokens stop working immediately.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
